# Game knowledge (from web research)

Collected from community guides and wikis on 2026-11-27. Sources disagree in
places (noted below); in-game observations in [game-notes.md](game-notes.md)
take precedence.

## Runs

- A main stage runs about 25 minutes; around minute 25 an **Extremis boss**
  appears that can only be beaten with a specific *evolved* weapon (e.g. Norn
  Emissary needs Gauntlets of Ultramar). Raw damage is not enough.
- Warhammer 40,000 and Age of Sigmar have separate coins, upgrades and unlocks.
- Objectives unlock items, e.g. surviving 1 minute (Tape Measure), 10 minutes
  (Hourglass); reaching level 20 unlocks the next stage. Completed objectives
  stay recorded even if you die.
- Imperial Bastion: rescuable characters (become followers) southeast and in
  the northeast corner; a Carnifex mini-boss; level 20 unlocks Gallowdark.

## Weapons and evolutions

- Weapons max out at **level 8**. An evolution needs the weapon at max level
  plus its matching passive item (some recipes need the passive at max level),
  then **opening a chest** dropped by a mini-boss (enemies with a **green
  outline**).
- Examples (40K): Chainsword + Tape Measure -> Eviscerator Chainsword,
  Boltgun + Dice Cube -> Heavy Bolter, Lasgun + Gauntlet -> Meltagun,
  Power Fist + Codex Astartes -> Gauntlets of Ultramar,
  Ardent Blade + Tactical Rock -> Weapon of the Matriarch,
  Power Klaw + Da Lucky Stikk -> Bale Eye,
  Storm Eagle Rocket + White Dwarf -> Deathstrike Missile,
  Nuln Oil + Hourglass -> Mordant Earth (shared with AoS).
- After an evolution has been discovered once, matching passives are marked on
  the level-up screen in later runs; the Armoury lists discovered recipes.
- Common advice: **focus on few weapons** and max them, and take their matching
  passives; spreading upgrades over many weapons is a typical mistake.

## Level-up tools

- Reroll (new choices), Skip (take XP instead), Banish (remove an item from the
  run, and you get nothing for that level). They are bought as Enhancements
  after being unlocked. Guides say Reroll is the most useful; Banish is a last
  resort.

## Enhancements (permanent, bought with coins)

| Enhancement | Per rank | Max | Base cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strength | +4% damage | +40% | 100 |
| Save | -0.5 incoming damage | -2.5 | 100 |
| Reload | 1% faster weapon use | 5% | 100 |
| Wounds | +4% max health (another source says +10%) | | 100 |
| Toughness | +0.1 health regen per second | 0.5/s | 300 |
| Sustain | +5% attack/effect duration | 25% | 300 |
| Range | +2% attack size | +10% | 300 |
| Attacks | +1 projectile for all weapons | +2 | 5000 |
| Skill | +4% projectile speed | 20% | 300 |
| Move | +3% movement speed | 15% | 300 |
| Aura | +10% pickup range | +50% | 300 |
| Chance | +5% luck (e.g. 4 level-up choices) | +25% | 300 |
| Veterancy | +2% experience | 20% | 400 |
| Tithe | +5% coins | +50% | 400 |
| **Corruption** | **+5% enemy speed, health, number** | +50% | 888 |
| Redeployment | one revive at 50% health | +2 | 10000 |

- Prices rise per rank. **Refund All is free**, so purchases can be undone.
- Guides disagree on the first buy: Strength (cheap, beginner guides) vs
  Attacks (strongest, but 5000+). Defensive picks (Save/Wounds/Toughness) help
  when dying to many small hits; Corruption makes the game harder and should
  not be bought by the bot.
- The final rank of an Enhancement unlocks only when it is maxed in both
  universes.

## Survival tips

- Don't stand still; move in wide circles, avoid dead ends, keep an escape
  route, give chargers and big monsters room.
- Don't chase skull gems (XP) into crowds; magnets collect everything later.
- Keep healing pickups for dangerous moments.
